The server checks every enabled subscription once an hour while it runs, and also records quota refreshes made in the console. History stays on this server for 14 days. Each limit is assumed full at the start of its reset cycle, and values between checks are interpolated. Totals add each selected limit's remaining percentage. The forecast continues each limit at its average daily pace (averaged over at least a full day, so a busy session is not projected around the clock) and refills it to 100% at its own reset. When a subscription runs out, its work moves to the provider's other subscriptions in proportion to their size. Size is measured from tokens the proxy routed per percent of quota used; until a subscription has used at least 5%, it is estimated from its plan (for example Max 20x = 20 × Pro, Codex Pro ≈ 6 × Plus). Use outside the proxy makes a subscription look smaller than it is.
