The server checks every enabled subscription once an hour while it runs, and also records quota refreshes made in the console. History stays on this server for 14 days. Each limit is assumed full at the start of its reset cycle, and values between checks are interpolated. A provider's pool adds each subscription's remaining percentage scaled by its size, so a subscription 2.5 times the base size counts as 250%. Burn rate is the consumption over the selected hours before each subscription's latest check. Future usage continues each limit at that burn, scaled as chosen (the 7-day average is spread over at least a full day so a busy session is not projected around the clock), and refills it at its own reset. Without a measured burn the 7-day average is used. When a subscription runs out, its work moves to the provider's other subscriptions in proportion to their size. Size is measured from tokens the proxy routed per percent of quota used; until a subscription has used at least 5%, it is estimated from its plan (for example Max 20x = 20 × Pro, Codex Pro ≈ 6 × Plus). Use outside the proxy makes a subscription look smaller than it is.
